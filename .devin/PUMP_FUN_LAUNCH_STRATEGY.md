# NAZUMI RONIN - PUMP.FUN LAUNCH STRATEGY

## Overview
This document outlines the complete strategy for launching NAZUMI RONIN on pump.fun, a Solana-based meme coin launchpad. The strategy focuses on community building, organic growth, and leveraging the Japanese manga aesthetic for differentiation.

## Pre-Launch Phase (2-4 weeks before launch)

### 1. Community Building
- **Discord Server**: Create and grow to 1,000+ members
- **Twitter/X**: Build following to 5,000+ followers
- **Telegram**: Create announcement channel
- **Content**: Daily posts about Japanese culture, ronin philosophy, project story
- **Engagement**: AMAs, Twitter Spaces, community polls

### 2. Marketing Preparation
- **Brand Assets**: Logo, banners, social media templates
- **Content Calendar**: 30-day content schedule
- **Influencer Outreach**: Identify and contact crypto influencers
- **Press Kit**: Prepare press release and media materials

### 3. Technical Setup
- **Pump.fun Account**: Create and verify account
- **Wallet Setup**: Prepare SOL wallet for launch
- **Metadata**: Finalize token name, symbol, description, image
- **Social Links**: Prepare website, Twitter, Discord links

## Launch Day Strategy

### 1. Launch Execution
- **Timing**: Launch during low-competition hours (avoid major crypto events)
- **Initial Buy**: Creator makes first buy (~0.02 SOL)
- **Price Monitoring**: Watch bonding curve progress
- **Community Support**: Encourage community to buy and share

### 2. Immediate Marketing
- **Social Media Blast**: Announce launch across all platforms
- **Influencer Notifications**: Alert partnered influencers
- **Community Engagement**: Pin launch announcement in Discord
- **Content Push**: Share launch across all channels

### 3. Volume Generation
- **Organic Trading**: Encourage community trading
- **Social Proof**: Share trading screenshots
- **Momentum Building**: Create FOMO through social media

## Post-Launch Phase (First 30 days)

### 1. Community Growth
- **Daily Updates**: Share project progress and milestones
- **Community Events**: Weekly AMAs, Twitter Spaces
- **Content Creation**: Regular blog posts, tutorials
- **Engagement**: Respond to community questions and feedback

### 2. Marketing Continuation
- **Influencer Partnerships**: Collaborate with crypto influencers
- **Content Marketing**: Blog posts, video content
- **PR Campaigns**: Crypto media outreach
- **Community Challenges**: Trading competitions, engagement campaigns

### 3. Graduation Push
- **Volume Focus**: Maintain consistent trading volume
- **Community Support**: Encourage continued participation
- **Social Proof**: Share progress toward graduation
- **Momentum**: Build anticipation for PumpSwap graduation

## Graduation Phase (~$69K market cap)

### 1. Graduation Celebration
- **Announcement**: Celebrate reaching graduation threshold
- **Community Recognition**: Thank community for support
- **Next Phase Planning**: Tease future developments

### 2. Post-Graduation Strategy
- **PumpSwap Trading**: Monitor trading on PumpSwap
- **Utility Development**: Begin planning token utilities
- **Community Growth**: Continue building community
- **Partnerships**: Explore ecosystem partnerships
