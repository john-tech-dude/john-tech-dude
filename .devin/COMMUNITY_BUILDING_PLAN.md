# NAZUMI RONIN - COMMUNITY BUILDING PLAN

## Overview
This document outlines the community building strategy for NAZUMI RONIN's pump.fun launch. The focus is on organic growth, Japanese cultural appeal, and creating a passionate community around the ronin narrative.

## Community Platforms

### 1. Discord Server
**Purpose**: Primary community hub for discussion, announcements, and engagement

**Setup**:n- Create Discord server with Japanese aesthetic theme
- Set up channels: #announcements, #general, #trading, #community, #support
- Add bots for moderation and engagement
- Create roles for community members

**Growth Strategy**:
- Daily engagement posts
- Weekly AMAs with team
- Community challenges and events
- User-generated content promotion
- Japanese culture discussions

**Target**: 1,000+ members by launch, 5,000+ by month 3

### 2. Twitter/X Account
**Purpose**: Public-facing announcements, content sharing, and community engagement

**Content Strategy**:
- Daily posts about Japanese culture and ronin philosophy
- Project updates and milestones
- Community spotlights and features
- Educational content about crypto and Solana
- Visual content with manga aesthetic

**Engagement Tactics**:
- Reply to relevant crypto conversations
- Use trending hashtags strategically
- Share community content
- Run Twitter Spaces AMAs
- Collaborate with influencers

**Target**: 5,000+ followers by launch, 20,000+ by month 6

### 3. Telegram Channel
**Purpose**: Quick announcements and real-time updates

**Content**:
- Launch announcements
- Price alerts and updates
- Community event notifications
- Important project news

**Target**: 2,000+ subscribers by launch
