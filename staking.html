OVERVIEW

修
What is Staking?

Staking allows you to lock your NAZUMI tokens in a secure smart contract to earn rewards. The longer you stake, the greater your rewards and access to exclusive clan benefits.

Think of it as warrior training — the more you dedicate yourself to the path, the stronger you become and the more wisdom you unlock.

行
Why Stake?

Staking aligns your interests with the clan's growth. By committing your tokens, you:

  • Earn Rewards: Receive NAZUMI tokens for staking
  • Unlock Lore: Access hidden Clan Scrolls chapters
  • Gain Status: Climb Clan Ranks for exclusive benefits
  • Shape the Story: Vote on lore directions and community decisions
  • Reduce Supply: Help stabilize the token through reduced circulation

HOW IT WORKS

一
Step 1: Connect Wallet

Connect your Solana wallet to the staking platform. We support all major wallets including Phantom, Solflare, and Backpack.

二
Step 2: Choose Pool

Select your staking pool based on your commitment level. Choose flexible staking or lock periods for higher rewards.

三
Step 3: Stake Tokens

Enter the amount of NAZUMI you wish to stake and confirm the transaction. Your tokens are secured in a non-custodial smart contract.

四
Step 4: Earn & Unlock

Rewards accrue automatically. Claim them anytime, unlock Clan Scrolls content, and climb the ranks of the Ronin Clan.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

問
Is staking safe?

Yes. Staking uses non-custodial smart contracts audited by CPK Solutions. Your tokens are held in Program Derived Addresses with no private keys. Nobody, including us, can access your staked tokens.

疑
Can I unstake anytime?

Flexible staking allows unstaking at any time. Locked staking requires waiting for the lock period to end, but you can claim rewards at any time without resetting your timer.

得
How are rewards calculated?

Rewards are distributed proportionally based on each staker's share of the pool. The more you stake and the longer you commit, the higher your rewards. APR adjusts dynamically based on total participation.

税
Are staking rewards taxable?

Staking rewards may be considered taxable income in your jurisdiction. Consult with a tax professional for guidance on your specific situation.
